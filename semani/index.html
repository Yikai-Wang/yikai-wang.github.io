Introduction

Entity-Level Text-Guided Image Manipulation (eL-TGIM) is a novel task of text-guided image manipulation on the entity level in the real world. eL-TGIM takes as inputs the entity prompt word, the target text description, and the original image from the real world. Below is an illustration of eL-TGIM.

eL-TGIM imposes three basic requirements,

  • To edit the entity consistent with the text descriptions;
  • To preserve the entity-irrelevant regions;
  • To merge the manipulated entity into the image naturally.
Most current image editors partially satisfy the first requirement while ignores the other two, especially the preservation of entity-irrelevant regions. Further, many editors rely on the user-provided mask to identify the entity-relevant region. Below are some corresponding examples.

Muse and Imagic generate new entity-irrelevant regions while DALLE2 requires user-provided mask to perform manipulation. Thus it is non-trivial to directly adopt current editors for eL-TGIM.

Framework

To solve eL-TGIM, we propose a framework, dubbed as SeMani, forming the Semantic Manipulation of real-world images. SeMani decomposes the task into two phases: the semantic alignment phase and the image manipulation phase. In the semantic alignment phase, SeMani incorporates a semantic alignment module to locate the image regions to be manipulated. In the image manipulation phase, SeMani adopts a generative model to synthesis new images conditioned on the unchanged regions and target descriptions. Below is an illustration of SeMani.

To implement SeMani, we resort to the two popular perspectives of viewing images, the discrete and the continuous perspectives. The discrete viewing is inspired by the auto-regressive transformers while the continuous viewing is inspired by the denoising diffusion probabilistic models, forming SeMani-Trans and SeMani-Diff, respectively. Each varient of SeMani adopts specific architectures and generation processes. SeMani can perform multiple-objects manipulation simultaneously or sequentially, as shown in below.

See our paper for details of SeMani-Trans and SeMani-Diff.
